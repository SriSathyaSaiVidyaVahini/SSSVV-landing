Sri Sathya Sai Vidya Vahini
18 – 19 September 2026

“Integral Education For All, By All”

No single institution has ever educated a nation. Realising the vision of NEP 2020 — holistic, inclusive, value-rooted education for every child — demands that the public, private, and philanthropic sectors build not in parallel, but together.

Sri Sathya Sai Vidya Vahini (SSSVV), a unit of the Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust, is committed to transforming education through meaningful, integral, inclusive, and technology-enabled initiatives — enriched by collaborations with esteemed institutions, government bodies, academic experts, and like-minded organisations across the nation.

The Collaborators Conference 2026 is an expression of our heartfelt gratitude to all our past, present, and future partners. Their expertise, guidance, trust, and support have been instrumental in our impactful work in the field of education at the national level.

This convening offers an opportunity to reflect on our journey from partnership to impact, showcase collaborative innovations and best practices, and engage in meaningful dialogue on the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead. Together, we will explore The Way Forward and strengthen our shared commitment to creating a lasting impact in education.
